# Synthetic Dataset Disclosure & Parameters

## Forty Years of Fees — Greenwich University Journey 40

### Research notice

Unified official fee registers for the complete 1987–2027 period are not available to this project. All generated fee amounts are illustrative synthetic estimates for product demonstration and methodology evaluation. They are not actual, audited, or verified Greenwich University prices. Do not cite them as institutional accounting records.

## Programme-specific scenario inputs

Every programme has a different starting amount, baseline annual growth rate, and fee component mix. These are modeling inputs selected to create distinct trajectories; they are not sourced historical quotes.

Annual growth also includes deterministic programme-level variation and shared broad economic-cycle adjustments. Fee components use programme-specific illustrative percentages. Rounded components sum to the total annual fee.

## Generated volume and refresh behavior

- 10 programmes × 41 years = 410 annual total fee records.
- 6 fee categories per programme-year = 2,400 total fee rows.
- CPI inputs are generated separately; see the inflation seed and methodology documentation.
- Re-running the seed refreshes generated fee rows for the configured programmes and preserves rows marked `VERIFIED`.

The scenario creates varied, repeatable data for demonstrating filters, comparisons, charts, and calculations. It cannot establish what any programme actually charged in a given year. Replace estimates with dated prospectuses, fee schedules, or other primary records and retain their provenance before treating any fee as historical fact.
