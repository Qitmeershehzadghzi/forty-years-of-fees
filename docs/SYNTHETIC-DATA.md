# Synthetic Dataset Disclosure & Parameters

## Forty Years of Fees — Greenwich University Journey 40

### Research Mandatory Notice
Continuous official historical fee registers covering the complete 40-year period (1987–2026) are not available in unified institutional archives. Therefore, a realistic, reproducible synthetic dataset has been algorithmically generated for demonstration, econometric testing, and methodology evaluation.

**Never represent generated figures as binding historical accounting records of Greenwich University.**

## 2. Component Decomposition
For each annual cycle, the total fee is partitioned into realistic administrative components:
- **Tuition Fee:** ~70%
- **Admission Fee:** ~12%
- **Examination Fee:** ~8%
- **Registration Fee:** ~5%
- **Other Charges:** Remainder

---

## 3. Total Generated Volume
- 10 Programmes × 40 Years = **400 Annual Total Tuition Records**
- 6 Component Categories × 400 = **2,400 Total Fee Items**
- 40 Years of National CPI Deflators (1987–2026)
