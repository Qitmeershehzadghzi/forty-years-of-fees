About the qualification

Pearson BTEC Higher National Diploma

Pearson is the United Kingdom's largest awarding body, and the BTEC Higher National Diploma (HND) is its flagship Level 5 qualification. Broadly equivalent to the first two years of a UK bachelor's degree. HNDs are career focused and hands on, designed with employers so what you study maps directly onto what industry actually needs.

Why Pearson HND

Why this pathway

Globally recognised

Pearson qualifications are accepted by universities and employers across more than 70 countries.

Degree top-up

Progress from an HND straight into the final year of a bachelor's degree. Abroad or here at Greenwich.

Practical by design

Assessment is coursework and project-based, not just exams. You build a real portfolio as you learn.
