Overview

Programme overview

The BA (Hons) Accounting and Finance programme is designed to prepare students for successful careers in accounting, banking, investment, financial management, and corporate finance. The curriculum combines technical accounting knowledge with financial analysis, business strategy, digital tools, and ethical decision making.

Students develop the practical and professional skills required to solve real world financial challenges while gaining exposure to industry practices, financial databases, simulations, and case studies.

Admissions

Who should apply

This is a 3-year full time programme.

Applicants should hold:

  • A-Levels
  • orFSc
  • orFA
  • orAn equivalent secondary school qualification

A background or interest in mathematics and quantitative subjects is beneficial.

Students with a relevant Pearson BTEC HND or equivalent diploma may be eligible for advanced entry, subject to programme requirements.

English proficiency is required.

This programme also offers a sandwich year option for eligible students who wish to undertake a period of professional work placement.

Outcomes

Programme Learning outcomes

Apply systematic and critical understanding of accounting and finance principles and theories.
Apply professional technical competencies and practices employed within organisations in the current accounting and finance context.
Apply competencies to formulate evidence-informed solutions to complex problems in challenging accounting and finance contexts.
Demonstrate professional communication skills across a variety of media.
Collaborate effectively in a team environment.
Be a reflective practitioner able to plan and develop your own professional future.
Understand professional ethical standards and responsibilities, and engage with broader social responsibilities as part of a global community.
